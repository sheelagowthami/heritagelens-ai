HeritageLens AI

Preserving Heritage Through AI-Powered Storytelling

HeritageLens AI is a Smart Tourism and Heritage Technology project that helps users explore historical places through AI-powered storytelling.

Problem Statement

Many historical monuments and heritage sites have information that is difficult to access, understand, or engage with. This limits cultural awareness and tourism experiences.

Solution

HeritageLens AI transforms heritage information into engaging AI-powered stories, providing historical insights, cultural knowledge, multilingual accessibility, and tourism recommendations.

Features

- AI-powered storytelling
- Heritage exploration
- Cultural preservation
- Tourism recommendations
- Multilingual support
